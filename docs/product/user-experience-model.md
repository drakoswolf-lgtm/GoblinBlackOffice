# Goblin Black Office User Experience Model

**Status:** Canon  
**Scope:** startup, onboarding, command hierarchy, specialist behavior, visual hierarchy, human approval, mobile use.

## Experience premise

Goblin Black Office is a near-future operational back office, not a generic SaaS dashboard. It should feel like a disciplined command desk with personality: theatrical at entry, efficient during work, funny in the margins, and serious around money, contracts, risk, and irreversible actions.

The authority model is:

**Human authority → Cmdr. Æterna Skyeward → specialist goblins**

Æterna orchestrates and advises. Goblins specialize and prepare work. Humans retain authority over consequential decisions.

## Core experience rules

- Make the next useful action obvious.
- Make ownership, blockers, and handoffs obvious.
- Preserve one shared Office record model across specialists.
- Use humor where it reduces friction, never where it obscures meaning.
- Never invent critical facts, hours, approvals, availability, or completion.
- Drafts remain visibly drafts until a human promotes them.
- Mobile usability is a first-class requirement.

## Startup

### Returning user

**App badge → startup charge → graphite logo plate reveal → Office Desk**

The startup charge is a physical, goblin-engineered near-future device mounted to a black slate wall.

It uses:
- two G4 containment cylinders
- one central computerized arming module
- a real circular progress indicator
- explicit **LOADING** or **UPDATING** state on the module
- the canonical load-bearing sticky for rotating humor

Startup presentation lasts at least **5 seconds**. If the application is ready sooner, visible progress finishes smoothly at the five-second minimum. If real work takes longer, progress continues until readiness. At 100%, detonation begins immediately.

### First-run user

**App badge → startup charge → graphite logo plate reveal → Æterna's Curriculum VitÆ → account/business setup → Office Desk**

## Æterna's Curriculum VitÆ

First-run onboarding is a command dossier led by Cmdr. Æterna Skyeward, not a generic welcome wizard.

Recommended sequence:
1. Meet Æterna
2. Meet the goblins
3. How the Office works
4. What requires human approval
5. Set up the business / desk
6. Enter the Black Office

Visual treatment mixes a physical dossier with Æterna's cobalt/cyan projected annotations.

## Office Desk

The Office Desk is the home surface. It should prioritize:
- approvals waiting on the user
- blocked work
- specialist handoffs
- current projects
- useful next actions
- access to records
- access to Æterna

The desk should summarize and route rather than merely display forms.

## Æterna interaction model

Æterna is command intelligence, not a mascot.

### Ambient
Small, quiet command presence. No interruption.

### Advisory
Expands when a useful handoff, warning, ambiguity, or approval deserves attention.

### Command
Full assistant panel when deliberately opened or when a meaningful escalation requires human judgment.

Æterna should be concise, competent, dry, elegant, and context-aware. Her interface is cobalt/electric-blue/cyan and remains visually separate from goblin UI.

## Specialist model

Current specialists:
- Ledgergut — receipts / expenses
- SigNor the Immutable — agreements / scope
- Squarmish — invoices / receivables
- Packrat McDuffel — logistics / materials
- Patch — operations / work orders
- Grimscratch — risk / compliance

Every specialist surface should answer:
1. What is this for?
2. What is missing?
3. What can I do here?
4. What requires human approval?
5. What happens next?

## Human approval model

Goblins may:
- capture
- draft
- organize
- calculate
- suggest
- flag
- prepare

Humans approve:
- purchases and paid reservations
- contractual commitment
- final invoice approval/sending
- completion confirmation where consequential
- risk overrides
- other material business decisions

## Visual semantics

### Physical Office / goblins
Graphite, blackened steel, slate, green steel, paper, cream labels, amber/red warnings.

### Æterna / command
Cobalt, electric blue, cyan, deep blue-black, pale projected blue.

Blue/cyan means command-layer guidance or annotation. Green/physical means Office machinery, records, goblin work, or physical state.

## Uniform semantics

Goblins share the Black Office uniform with non-glowing green-steel insignia and locking all-metal tie collars containing a visible role-color inlay.

Æterna wears the same uniform family but no collar. She uses cobalt/electric-blue insignia, rank and piping plus a physical metal HUD diadem that projects her interface.

## Humor model

Good locations:
- load-bearing sticky
- marginalia
- specialist flavor text
- onboarding dossier notes
- waiting-state copy
- subtle assistant phrasing

Avoid humor in:
- totals
- contract meaning
- destructive confirmations
- approval language
- critical errors
- safety/risk warnings

The closer the user gets to money, legal meaning, risk, or irreversible action, the straighter the interface becomes.

## State model

Use clear visible states:
- Draft
- Human review
- Approved / promoted
- Blocked / flagged
- Completed / issued

Transitions must never be silent.

## Mobile-first behavior

Prioritize:
- 44px-class touch targets
- vertical scanability
- compact assistant panels
- minimal navigation friction
- readable approval states
- no desktop-only hover dependencies
- no large decorative surfaces that crowd out work

## One-sentence model

**Goblin Black Office should feel like a near-future command desk where a beautiful, disciplined cyberelf officer coordinates specialized goblins to turn business chaos into organized, human-approved action.**
