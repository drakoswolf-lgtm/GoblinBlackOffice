# G4 Startup Charge Visual Specification

**Status:** Canon  
**Purpose:** production visual reference for the Goblin Black Office startup/loading splash.

## 1. Silhouette and composition

The charge is a compact, wall-mounted, three-part device:

**G4 CYLINDER | ARMING COMPUTER | G4 CYLINDER**

The side cylinders are vertically oriented and slightly taller than the center module. The center computer is visually dominant because it carries the progress display and the load-bearing sticky.

The entire assembly mounts to a blackened graphite frame bolted directly to the slate wall. The silhouette should feel symmetrical at first glance, with enough goblin-built irregularity in hoses, clamps, fasteners, service labels, and repairs to keep it from looking mass-produced.

It must look high-tech, rugged, militarized, and professionally improvised.

It must not resemble a cartoon bomb, antique explosive, pipe bomb, magic artifact, or consumer electronics device.

## 2. G4 cylinders

Two armored transparent containment cylinders flank the computer.

Contents are the proprietary **G4 blend**:

- Goblanium
- Gobloleum
- Tri-Glob
- Liquid Goblium
- Copper Gloxide additive

**Proprietary blend. Smells minty.**

Copper Gloxide is fictional in-world chemistry. Its visual role is to provide a metallic copper filament/swirl within the luminous green G4 and support the aquamarine-green detonation language.

### Cylinder construction
- blackened metal end caps
- mechanical locking collars
- reinforced transparent containment body
- narrow protective ribs / cage elements
- small pressure/status glyphs
- armored hose connection into the center module
- no exposed fragile glass aesthetic

### Fluid appearance
The G4 reads as a dense luminous green suspension, not plain liquid.

Inside each cylinder:
- bright green body glow
- darker emerald depth
- slowly rotating turbulent flow
- thin metallic-copper ribbons/filaments
- occasional brighter green eddies
- subtle particulate or microbubble movement

The copper remains secondary. The cylinder reads green first, copper second.

## 3. Central arming computer

The center module is a compact hardened computer with:
- graphite/blackened metal enclosure
- protected corners
- visible fasteners
- small vents / service seams
- one dominant rectangular screen
- minimal physical controls
- goblin-made labels / inspection marks used sparingly

The screen is not a timer.

### Display
The screen always contains:
- a circular progress ring
- numeric percentage if space permits
- one functional state word:
  - **LOADING**
  - **UPDATING**

The visual hierarchy is:
1. progress ring
2. percentage
3. state word

No countdown appears.

## 4. Progress-linked behavior

Visible splash duration is at least **5 seconds**.

### 0–24%
- restrained cylinder motion
- lower green luminance
- copper threads slow and clearly visible
- calm progress ring

### 25–49%
- brighter G4
- mild turbulence increase
- occasional light transmission through hoses
- screen remains stable

### 50–79%
- visibly energetic circulation
- copper filaments stretch into longer spirals
- green light begins to reflect onto nearby graphite and slate
- no shaking or unstable cartoon behavior

### 80–99%
- G4 reaches highest contained brightness
- turbulence becomes menacing but controlled
- aquamarine highlights begin appearing around copper-bearing eddies
- center module may show tiny electrical edge flickers
- charge remains mechanically stable

### 100%
Detonation begins immediately. No joke completion, pause, or fake countdown.

## 5. Load-bearing sticky

The sticky is physically attached to the **central computer enclosure**, offset from the screen so it never blocks the progress display.

Static red header:

**LOAD-BEARING STICKY**  
**DO NOT REMOVE**

Below it is the canonical rotating joke copy.

Visual behavior:
- paper remains physically fixed
- slight corner lift or vibration is acceptable
- a very subtle irregular light flicker may pass across it during loading
- the text itself should not glitch
- the sticky must remain readable

The sticky is the humor delivery device. The computer is the functional status device.

## 6. Mounting and goblin engineering details

The device should reward a closer look without becoming cluttered.

Approved details:
- mismatched but compatible fasteners
- one or two hand-engraved service marks
- compact armored hoses
- practical clamps
- small warning chevrons
- tiny inspection stamp
- one repair plate or replacement fastener
- asymmetry in non-critical hardware

Avoid:
- dangling wires
- unsafe-looking exposed conductors
- random junk glued to the device
- excessive gauges
- steampunk brass
- fantasy runes

The joke is that goblins built it. The visual must still imply they are very good at their jobs.

## 7. Color and material

### Body
- graphite
- blackened steel
- dark gunmetal
- matte / satin surfaces
- limited edge wear

### G4
- luminous emerald green
- acid green highlights
- aquamarine near peak load

### Copper Gloxide
- metallic copper / warm copper
- reflective rather than glowing orange

### Display
- green/aquamarine functional graphics
- high contrast
- crisp and modern

### Sticky
- warm cream/yellow paper
- red stamped header
- dark handwritten/printed joke text

## 8. Detonation

The event is a compact controlled breach, not a spherical fireball.

Sequence:
1. progress reaches 100%
2. central screen flashes completion for only a fraction of a beat
3. G4 cylinders spike bright green/aquamarine
4. ignition propagates inward toward the center module
5. compact **aquamarine-green flame front** bursts across the slate
6. slate fractures and clears
7. dust and glowing particles dissipate rapidly
8. a graphite logo plate is revealed behind the wall

Flame character:
- green dominant
- aquamarine core/highlights
- very limited warm copper sparks
- no red/orange generic explosion
- no electric-blue plasma cloud
- no magical green fog

The effect should read as chemical/engineered even though the chemistry is fictional.

## 9. Graphite plate reveal

Behind the slate wall is a dark graphite architectural plate with:
- the canonical angular right-facing bird insignia
- controlled green illuminated lines
- Goblin Black Office wordmark
- restrained backlighting / edge lighting
- physical fasteners or flush mounting details

The logo plate is not floating and not magical. It is physically installed behind the sacrificial slate face.

## 10. Mobile framing

Primary composition is portrait-safe:
- central computer stays within the middle third
- cylinders remain fully readable on narrow screens
- sticky copy remains legible
- progress ring is large enough to read without zoom
- wall/floor perspective remains subtle so the charge owns the frame

The design should also crop gracefully to wider desktop/tablet layouts.

## 11. Production test

Reject a future rendering if:
- the cylinders look like ordinary bottles
- the computer looks like a kitchen timer
- the sticky blocks the display
- the copper dominates the G4 green
- the charge looks crude rather than goblin-engineered
- the device resembles a real-world improvised weapon
- the flame becomes orange/red
- the detonation becomes magical fog
- the wall reveal does not expose a physical graphite logo plate
- the splash can complete visually in under five seconds
