# Goblin Black Office Visual Canon

**Status:** CANON  
**Applies to:** brand identity, app badge, splash/loading experience, onboarding, uniforms, specialist portraits, Cmdr. Æterna Skyeward, in-app assistant UI, future marketing art.

This document records approved visual decisions. When concept art conflicts with this document, this document wins.

---

## 1. Core visual language

Goblin Black Office is **militaristic, industrial, near-future software**, not fantasy cosplay and not whimsical cartoon goblins.

The shared physical environment is built from:
- black and graphite surfaces
- dark steel
- slate
- restrained green-steel accents
- practical hardware
- paper, labels, stamps, tools, fasteners, and other physical office/workshop artifacts

The product may be funny, but the hardware and uniforms should look credible, engineered, and professional.

Humor belongs in details, copy, marginalia, employee behavior, and props. It should not make the core design look unserious.

---

## 2. Master insignia

The approved insignia is a **minimal, angular, isometric bird mark**.

### Geometry rules
- Maximum **8 primary vector shapes** in the master construction.
- Simple enough to reproduce by hand from memory.
- Instantly recognizable at small size.
- Strong silhouette before any texture, lighting, or color is applied.
- Built from hard, deliberate, isometric/angular geometry rather than soft curves.
- The **bird head looks to the right**.
- The head must read clearly as a bird head, not a generic chevron, phoenix, goose, or winged shield.
- The body/wing geometry should feel forward-moving and engineered.
- The design must remain legible as:
  - a single-color vector
  - a small app badge
  - a uniform insignia
  - an embossed equipment mark
  - a metal badge
  - a luminous UI mark

### Canon variants
All variants use the **same geometry**. Do not redraw the bird for different uses.

1. **Goblin / physical primary**  
   Non-glowing green steel.

2. **Neutral / embossed**  
   Graphite, blackened steel, or unlit metal.

3. **Æterna / command**  
   Cobalt-blue / electric-blue illuminated treatment. Same insignia, command material language.

4. **UI / luminous**  
   Controlled glow derived from the appropriate owner palette. Never change the geometry to create the glow.

### Rejected directions
The following are explicitly non-canon:
- phoenix-style spread-wing crests
- rounded or feathery bird marks
- bird silhouettes that read as a goose
- highly detailed heraldry
- marks that need texture or glow to be recognizable

---

## 3. App badge

The app badge uses the master insignia in its simplest form.

Requirements:
- same approved bird geometry
- no text required at icon size
- dark graphite / black field
- strong silhouette
- restrained green-steel or green luminous treatment
- must remain readable at very small mobile launcher sizes

The badge is not a separate logo family.

---

## 4. Shared uniform system

Æterna and the goblins wear the **same underlying Black Office uniform family**.

The uniform should read as:
- near-future military / command utility wear
- black or graphite
- structured and tailored
- functional rather than ornamental
- credible as workwear for a disciplined specialist unit

The shared cut establishes that everyone belongs to the same Office.

The differences in insignia, piping, rank treatment, collar hardware, and projection technology establish role and hierarchy.

The **approved new bird insignia must appear on the uniforms**. Older crest variants are not to be used.

---

## 5. Goblin uniforms

All goblins wear the shared Black Office uniform.

### Goblin material language
- insignia: **non-glowing green steel**
- rank and piping: green-steel / physical green accents
- hardware: physical metal
- wear may be more practical, battered, repaired, or individualized than Æterna's uniform

### Collars
All goblins wear a **metal collar that visually functions as a tie beneath the uniform**.

The canonical direction is the **locking-style metal tie collar**.

Rules:
- entirely metal in construction
- looks engineered and lockable
- sits where a conventional tie would sit
- integrates naturally under the uniform collar
- includes a deliberate **role-color insert / inlay / indicator**
- the color area must remain visible even when the rest of the collar is dark or metallic
- role-color mapping is a separate design decision and is not yet fixed here

Other collar concepts may exist as exploratory designs, but the locking metal tie collar is the current canonical default.

Æterna does **not** wear a collar.

---

## 6. Cmdr. Æterna Skyeward

**Role:** Command / orchestration / executive function / human-facing AI assistant.

Æterna is not a goblin and should never be visually mistaken for one.

### Character
She is a cyberelf command officer:
- female
- short silver military haircut
- subtly pointed ears
- attractive, authoritative, composed, and self-possessed
- physically present / corporeal first
- technological projection effects second

She should be beautiful and sexually appealing without becoming a pin-up or undermining her credibility as a soldier and commanding officer.

The design priority is:

**soldier first, command presence second, beauty and sex appeal through confidence, tailoring, posture, gaze, and precision.**

Avoid:
- fantasy-elf styling
- excessive exposed skin
- exaggerated hologram transparency
- ornamental sci-fi costume design
- anything that makes her look decorative rather than operational

### Command palette
Æterna's visual palette stays **separate from the goblin palette**.

Her elements use:
- glowing cobalt blue
- electric blue
- cyan
- deep blue-black
- pale holographic blue where needed

This applies to:
- insignia
- rank marks
- piping
- HUD
- advice boxes
- notes
- assistant panels
- active command indicators
- projection elements

Her command UI should never default to goblin green.

### Insignia and rank
Æterna wears the same master bird insignia geometry as the Office, but:
- illuminated cobalt/electric blue
- command rank and piping use the same blue family
- restrained glow is acceptable because it identifies command authority

---

## 7. Æterna's HUD diadem

Æterna does **not** wear a goblin collar.

She wears a **physical metal command diadem**.

The diadem:
- is clearly metallic and corporeal
- reads as military command hardware, not jewelry
- may sit across or around the upper forehead/head
- contains emitters/projectors
- projects a cyan/electric-blue HUD around her
- creates screens, targeting-style overlays, status panes, or command data in controlled arcs around the head
- should look engineered enough that the projected interface appears to come from real hardware

The HUD itself may be luminous and spatial, but Æterna should remain visibly corporeal beneath it.

The diadem is her command equivalent to the goblins' collars.

---

## 8. Æterna as the in-app assistant

Æterna is the persistent human-facing intelligence of the Black Office.

She is the equivalent of a deeply useful assistant character, but must never behave like an intrusive mascot.

Presence levels:
- **Ambient:** small, quiet avatar / command presence
- **Advisory:** expands when useful context or a handoff deserves attention
- **Command:** deliberate user-opened conversation / decision interface

Her boxes, notes, recommendations, and prompts use her cobalt/cyan/electric-blue palette.

Goblin-originated UI remains physical/green/graphite unless Æterna is actively presenting or annotating it.

She should remain concise, competent, dry, and context-aware.

## 9. Loading / startup splash

The returning-user startup scene is physical, industrial, and near-future.

### Environment
- mostly a black slate wall
- slight slate floor / lower plane for depth
- restrained industrial lighting
- logo hidden behind the wall

### Goblin bomb
The bomb is:
- high-tech
- compact
- engineered
- visibly built or modified by goblins
- slightly overcomplicated in believable hardware details
- physical, not magical
- attached to the slate wall

It must not look like:
- a cartoon bomb
- a generic pipe bomb
- a fantasy explosive
- a glowing magical artifact

### Progress
The bomb carries a real **loading ring**.

Rules:
- ring represents actual loading/update progress
- no fake countdown
- no redundant functional `Loading...` copy is required
- at 100%, the explosion begins immediately
- do not delay detonation to finish a joke

### Load-bearing sticky
A physical sticky note is attached to the bomb.

Permanent red text:

**LOAD-BEARING STICKY**  
**DO NOT REMOVE**

Only the joke beneath it changes.

The production joke pool, progress ranges, probabilities, and rotation rules are canonical in:
- `app/office/loading_sticky.py`
- `docs/brand/load-bearing-sticky.md`

## 10. Explosion and logo reveal

The loading explosion is **physical demolition**, not magical spectacle.

At 100%:
1. the goblin bomb detonates immediately
2. slate fractures and clears away
3. debris/dust/energy remain controlled and near-future
4. the wall reveals a **graphite plate beneath it**
5. the graphite plate contains illuminated design elements
6. the approved bird insignia and Goblin Black Office identity are revealed on or within that plate
7. the revealed plate becomes the bridge into the application

The reveal must not use:
- a magical green cloud
- fantasy flames
- mystical energy as the primary reveal mechanism
- a different logo than the approved master insignia

Green illumination may be used as engineered light, status illumination, electrical discharge, or backlighting.

## 11. First-run onboarding: Æterna's Curriculum VitÆ

New users receive an additional onboarding sequence after the logo reveal.

Working/canonical title:

# Æterna's Curriculum VitÆ

Purpose:
- introduce Cmdr. Skyeward
- establish the Black Office hierarchy
- introduce the goblins
- explain what the Office does
- explain human approval boundaries
- move the user into initial business setup

### Visual treatment
The onboarding should combine:
- physical Black Office dossier / file / document language
- graphite and paper surfaces
- stamps, annotations, and personnel-file structure
- Æterna's cyan/cobalt projected annotations layered over the physical material

This is the intentional meeting point between:
- the goblins' physical world
- Æterna's command interface

### Suggested orientation sequence
1. Meet Æterna
2. Meet the goblins
3. How the Office works
4. Human authority / approval boundaries
5. Your business / desk setup
6. Enter the Black Office

The exact copy remains editable. The concept and visual hierarchy are canonical.

## 12. Palette separation rule

This is a hard rule.

### Goblins / physical Office
Use:
- graphite
- blackened steel
- slate
- green steel
- restrained green illumination
- paper / cream where physical documents are appropriate
- amber/red for ordinary warning/danger states

### Æterna / command
Use:
- cobalt
- electric blue
- cyan
- deep blue-black
- pale projected blue

Do not casually mix the two palettes.

When the palettes meet, the reason should be obvious:
- Æterna annotating a goblin record
- Æterna appearing over a physical dossier
- command intervention in the physical Office
- a shared insignia rendered in the material language of its wearer

## 13. Brand hierarchy

The intended hierarchy is:

**Goblin Black Office**  
↳ **Cmdr. Æterna Skyeward** — command, orchestration, human-facing intelligence  
↳ **Goblin specialists** — physical specialist workforce

Current specialists:
- Ledgergut — receipts / expenses
- SigNor the Immutable — agreements / scope
- Squarmish — invoices / receivables
- Packrat McDuffel — logistics / materials
- Patch — operations / work orders
- Grimscratch — risk / compliance

Æterna coordinates. Goblins specialize. Humans retain authority over consequential decisions.

## 14. Non-canon / superseded visual directions

Do not reintroduce these without an explicit design change:
- phoenix or spread-wing heraldic logo
- “glowing goose” bird geometry
- complex multi-feather insignia
- Æterna wearing a goblin collar
- Æterna rendered primarily as a transparent hologram
- long/fantasy-styled silver hair
- magical green explosion clouds
- generic old-fashioned bomb design
- goblin uniforms using old crest geometry
- goblin collars without a visible role-color area
- cyan/blue goblin interface as the default
- green Æterna command UI as the default

---

## 15. Design test

Before approving a future visual, ask:

1. Does the mark still read with no glow or texture?
2. Is the bird head unmistakably facing right?
3. Could the insignia plausibly be drawn from memory?
4. Is the scene near-future and militaristic rather than fantasy?
5. Are goblins physically green-steel and collared?
6. Is Æterna clearly command-class, collarless, and using the metal HUD diadem?
7. Is Æterna's cobalt/cyan palette visually separate from the goblins?
8. Does Æterna look like a credible commanding officer before she looks like a model?
9. If this is the splash, is progress real and detonation immediate at 100%?
10. Is the latest approved insignia geometry actually present on the uniform/equipment?

If any answer is no, the visual is not on-model.
